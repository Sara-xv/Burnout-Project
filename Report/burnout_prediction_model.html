Why Custom Thresholds Were Needed

burnout_risk is imbalanced (Moderate=66%, Low=18%, High=16%). With default predictions, the model correctly identifies only 26% of real High-risk employees — it defaults to predicting "Moderate" too often. Missing a real High-risk employee is costlier than a false alarm, so custom decision thresholds were applied: an employee is flagged High if P(High) exceeds a threshold, even when it isn't the single most likely class.

Role-Based Thresholds

Manager and Executive roles have the highest actual High-risk rates in this dataset (25.7% and 20.3% respectively, vs 11-17% for other roles — see earlier EDA), so they use a more sensitive threshold (P(High) ≥ 0.2) than other roles (P(High) ≥ 0.25).
